I get up at seven o'clock every day. First I take a shower. Then I drink a cup of coffee in the kitchen. I usually eat bread with cheese.

At eight o'clock I cycle to my work. It is not far, but when it rains, I take the bus. At work I say good morning to my colleagues.

At the weekend my morning is different. I sleep late and I have a relaxed breakfast with my girlfriend. Then we tidy up the house together.
